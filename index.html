Research

Residual RL Gait Compensation for a Hexapod with Partial Leg Degradation
Mar 2026 – Present · First Author & Project Lead · Advisor: Prof. Zhengang Lu
Manuscript in preparation for submission to BIROB

Developed independent residual PPO controllers for tripod, tetrapod, and wave gaits using six interpretable gait variables. Built a MuJoCo-to-MiniHexa pipeline with a four-stage curriculum and transferred the learned policies to 50-Hz hardware without retraining.

Across 45 hardware trials with the left-front leg limited to 20% actuation, the learned compensation reduced mean final lateral deviation from 23.0 cm to 7.2 cm. The curriculum-trained policy recovered in 13/15 trials, compared with 7/15 for direct training and 0/15 without training.

White-Image Calibration and Overlap-Aware Processing for a Light-Field Camera
Sep 2025 – Mar 2026 · Research Project

Implemented forward and inverse white-image calibration workflows, including image acquisition, preprocessing, thresholding, contour fitting, connected-component analysis, and Hough-circle detection for low- and high-overlap microlens arrays.

Generated microlens-center arrays and evaluated localization methods by accuracy, operating conditions, and failure modes to support downstream sub-aperture extraction, defect detection, and 3D reconstruction.
