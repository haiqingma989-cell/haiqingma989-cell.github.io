Research

Gait Compensation Using Reinforcement Learning for a Hexapod Robot with Partial Leg Degradation
First author · Manuscript in preparation

Developed a structured residual PPO approach for compensating partial actuator degradation while preserving the original gait structure. The learned controller adjusts a compact set of interpretable gait parameters and is transferred from MuJoCo simulation to a physical hexapod robot for hardware validation.

Current Research Direction

I am interested in extending my current work toward robust and adaptive reinforcement learning for robots operating under changing dynamics. In particular, I am interested in combining learned adaptation with structured robot control, online system estimation, and physical validation.
