Research

Residual Reinforcement Learning for Hexapod Gait Compensation
Mar 2026 – Present · First Author & Project Lead · Advisor: Prof. Zhengang Lu

Developed a structured residual reinforcement learning method for partial leg actuation degradation. Separate PPO policies for tripod, tetrapod, and wave gaits adjust six interpretable gait variables while preserving the prescribed coordination among the legs.

Built a MuJoCo-to-MiniHexa training and deployment pipeline using staged curriculum learning and fixed calibration for simulation-to-hardware transfer. Compared curriculum training with direct PPO under the same interaction budget.

Validated the method in 45 hardware trials under severe front-left-leg degradation, reducing average terminal lateral deviation by 68.7% across three gait patterns.

White Image Calibration and Overlap Aware Processing for a Light Field Camera
Sep 2025 – Present · Research Project

Developed an overlap-aware white-image calibration pipeline for microlens arrays, combining thresholding, watershed segmentation, and Delaunay-based missing-center restoration.

Estimated microlens radii using midpoint gray-level scanning and evaluated center and radius estimation over an overlap-ratio range of 0–30%.

Currently investigating EPI-based depth estimation and 3D reconstruction, including multi-feature line-structure analysis, sub-pixel disparity refinement, depth mapping, and point-cloud reconstruction from light-field images.
